# 🕌 Islamic Finance & Shariah Compliance News Hub

A free, browser-based dashboard that aggregates live, real-time news and opportunities relevant to the global Islamic finance and Shariah compliance community — all in a single page, with no installation, login, or backend server required.

## 📖 About

This tool was built to make it easier for professionals in Islamic banking, Takaful, Sukuk markets, and Shariah advisory roles to stay current — without needing to check multiple news sites individually. It runs entirely in your browser and pulls live content each time you open or refresh the page.

## ✨ Features

- **📰 News** — Curated headlines across 8 categories: Global Islamic Finance, Sukuk Markets, Takaful, Shariah Compliance & Standards, Murabaha/Ijarah/Musharakah contracts, Pakistan Islamic Finance, GCC & Gulf Islamic Banking, and Islamic Fintech.
- **🤝 Business Opportunities** — News relevant to trade, exports, distribution, manufacturing, SME/Islamic trade financing, GCC business openings, Halal industry expansion, government tenders, and Sukuk-financed infrastructure projects — geared toward Pakistani businesses looking to grow locally and abroad.
- **💼 Careers** — Open job listings and hiring-related news in Islamic banking, finance, and Takaful, filterable by region: Pakistan, Middle East/GCC, Southeast Asia, South Asia, UK & Europe, and Global/Remote.
- **Search & filter** across all tabs, keyword highlighting, auto-refresh every 30 minutes, and a built-in diagnostics panel that explains clearly if a source fails to load (e.g., due to network restrictions).

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the live page (see the GitHub Pages link in this repository's "About" section, right sidebar).
2. Use the top navigation to switch between **News**, **Business Opportunities**, and **Careers**.
3. Use the category tabs / region buttons to narrow results, or type in the search box to filter by keyword.
4. Click **Refresh** anytime to pull the latest updates.

## 🛠️ How It Works

The page fetches live RSS feeds (Google News searches for industry headlines and business opportunities; Indeed regional feeds for job openings, with a news-based fallback where job-board feeds are unavailable) using the free `rss2json.com` bridge, with an automatic CORS-proxy fallback if needed. Everything runs client-side in your browser — there is no server, database, or paid API involved.
