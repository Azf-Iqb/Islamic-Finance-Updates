How this works: This page pulls live RSS searches (Google News for industry headlines and business opportunities; Indeed regional feeds for job openings, with a news-based fallback where job-board feeds are unavailable), converts them via the free rss2json.com bridge (with an automatic CORS-proxy fallback), then merges, de-duplicates and sorts everything by date. No installation, login, or server needed.
